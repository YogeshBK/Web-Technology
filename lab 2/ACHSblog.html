Sports Week

Sports Week is a special program organized to encourage students to take part in physical activities and outdoor games. It provides a platform for students to display their skills and learn the importance of cooperation and team spirit.

Various indoor and outdoor games are conducted during Sports Week, allowing students to explore their interests beyond academics. Participation in sports helps students stay active, build confidence, and reduce stress.

The event also encourages fair play and healthy competition. It creates memorable moments and promotes a healthy lifestyle among students.
